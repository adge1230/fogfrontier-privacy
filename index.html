《迷雾边境》隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 16 日

《迷雾边境》（Fog Frontier）是由以 Oops Studio 名义运营的个人开发者发行的离线 Android 游戏。本政策适用于 Android 正式版（org.fogfrontier.prototype）。

游戏处理的信息

游戏不会为开发者收集个人信息，也不会将个人信息或游玩数据发送到服务器。游戏不要求提供姓名或邮箱，不追踪现实位置，不使用广告 ID 或其他持久设备标识符，也不提供游戏账号。

本地存档与偏好设置

游戏将进度、已探索区域、游戏内坐标、任务、背包和语言、音量、显示等设置保存在设备上的应用私有存储中，用于恢复游戏和偏好设置。本地备份用于在存档写入中断后恢复。你主动导出或导入存档时，游戏会打开文件选择器，由你选择写入或读取的位置。游戏不提供云存档或跨设备同步。

网络连接与第三方服务

游戏可离线游玩。Android 正式版不会发起游戏网络请求，也不使用广告、统计分析、远程崩溃报告或应用内购买 SDK。应用商店可能依据各自的隐私政策处理账户、付款和下载信息。游戏不会获得你的付款详情。本政策网页本身不会设置 Cookie，也不会运行追踪代码。托管服务商可能为提供网页及保障服务安全而处理常规访问请求信息。

保存与删除

本地存档和设置保留在设备上，直至被删除或替换。如需删除游戏的私有数据，可在 Android 系统设置中对《迷雾边境》执行「清除存储」，或卸载游戏。这会删除本地进度和设置。此前主动导出的副本需要在保存位置另行删除。开发者没有可远程删除的游戏存档副本。

儿童隐私

游戏不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童通过客服消息向开发者提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

政策变更

政策更新将在本页面公布，并修改上方的更新日期。如有重大变更，也会在相关版本说明或商店页面告知。

联系方式

如有隐私问题或请求，请通过 linsg730@gmail.com 联系 Oops Studio。如果你发邮件联系我们，我们会使用你的邮箱地址和你主动提供的信息回复，并仅在处理请求或履行法律义务所需的期限内保留通信记录。

Fog Frontier Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Fog Frontier (《迷雾边境》) is an offline Android game published by the independent developer operating under the name Oops Studio. This policy describes the Android release (org.fogfrontier.prototype).

Information the game handles

The game does not collect personal information for the developer or send personal information or gameplay data to a server. It does not ask for your name or email address, track real-world location, use advertising IDs or other persistent device identifiers, or provide a game account.

Local saves and preferences

The game stores progress, explored areas, in-game position, quests, inventory and settings such as language, audio and display preferences in its private storage on your device. It uses this information to restore your game and preferences. Local backup copies help the game recover from interrupted saves. When you choose to export or import a save, the game opens a file picker so you can select where to write or read the file. The game does not provide cloud saves or cross-device synchronization.

Network connections and third parties

Gameplay works offline. The Android release makes no game network requests and uses no advertising, analytics, remote crash reporting or in-app purchase SDK. App stores may process account, payment and download information for their own services under their own privacy policies. This game does not receive your payment details. This policy page itself sets no cookies and runs no tracking code. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information to deliver and secure the page.

Retention and deletion

Local saves and settings remain on your device until removed or replaced. To remove the game's private data, use Android's Clear storage option for Fog Frontier or uninstall the game. This removes local progress and settings. Copies you previously exported must be deleted separately at the location where you saved them. The developer has no remote copy of your game save to delete.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer through a support message, please contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We will post revisions here and update the date above. For significant changes, we will also note the change in the applicable release notes or store listing.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Oops Studio at linsg730@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond and retain the correspondence only as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.
